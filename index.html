Privacy Policy

We treat customer identity, enquiries and case-related information as confidential. We do not publicly disclose who contacted the agency or what enquiry they submitted, except where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Enquiry information is used to respond to the customer and assess the requested service. Access should be limited to authorized personnel. We take reasonable steps to protect submitted information and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for service and legal obligations.

Customers may request correction or deletion of their information, subject to applicable legal requirements. We do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing without appropriate permission.

Terms & Conditions

Customers must provide accurate information and a lawful reason for requesting an investigation. Customers must obtain any permissions or authorizations required for the requested service and cooperate with reasonable verification requests.

TAVORA will use only lawful and authorized methods. We do not undertake stalking, harassment, unauthorized access to accounts, devices or communications, or unlawful collection of private records.

Findings depend on the accuracy of information and lawful availability of records or evidence. No specific outcome or 100% verification result is guaranteed. Missing-person enquiries may require coordination with law-enforcement or other appropriate authorities.

Service availability and acceptance are subject to an initial review of the enquiry and applicable law.
